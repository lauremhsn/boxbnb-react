BOXBNB — Business Summary
"Find your box. Ignore your human."

ABOUT
Boxbnb is the world's leading short-stay rental platform for cats. We connect discerning cats with premium resting spots: cardboard boxes, sunbeams, laptop keyboards, freshly folded laundry, and the exact spot your human was about to sit in. Boxbnb was founded on a sunny third-floor windowsill by three cats who were tired of sleeping in places that were merely comfortable. Comfort is easy. Comfort that ruins someone's afternoon is an art.

MISSION
To give every cat access to a resting spot that is warm, enclosed, and as inconvenient as possible for the nearest human.

UNIQUE VALUE PROPOSITION
Boxbnb is the only platform where every listing is certified Maximally Inconvenient. Our Inconvenience Guarantee: if your human is not at least mildly annoyed by your stay, your next night is free.

SERVICES AND PRICING (all prices in treats)

Box Stays (per night)
- Shoebox Studio: 2 treats. Cozy, snug, slightly too small. Guests love it anyway.
- Prime Location Box (standard delivery box): 5 treats. Our most popular listing.
- Fridge Box Penthouse: 12 treats. Spacious, multi-level once you chew a door into it.

Sunbeam Timeshares (per hour)
- Living Room Sunbeam: 3 treats/hour, available 2pm-4pm only.
- Bedroom Sunbeam: 2 treats/hour, mornings only.
- All sunbeams are subject to clouds. No refunds for weather.

Premium Inconvenience Listings (per session)
- Keyboard Suite: 8 treats. Laptop keyboard, guaranteed to be booked during your human's deadlines.
- Fresh Laundry Pile: 6 treats. Warm, clean, dark clothing preferred for maximum fur visibility.
- The Doorway: 4 treats. Stand halfway in, halfway out. Do not commit.
- Human's Chair (still warm): 7 treats. Book the moment they get up.

Add-ons
- 4AM Wake-Up Service: 3 treats. A gentle paw to the face, repeated until results.
- Glass Edge Package: 5 treats. Includes one full glass of water placed near a table edge.
- Video Call Cameo: 4 treats. Walk across the screen during an important meeting.

POLICIES
- Check-in: whenever. Check-out: whenever. We do not believe in schedules.
- Cancellation: we don't care.
- Recycling Day Clause: Boxbnb is not responsible if your human recycles your box during your stay. No refunds.
- The bathtub listing is decorative and cannot be booked. Do not ask.
- Dogs are not permitted as guests.

A spot with 7 points or more is certified Maximally Inconvenient and gets listed. Anything less is rejected.
